Good to know

Questions.

The light

Blue blinking: waiting for setup. Green: connected. Breathing: searching.

The button

Short press: sleep and wake. Hold five seconds: factory reset.

No contact with the preamplifier

Check that its RS‑232 port is enabled and set to 115200 baud, and that the cable is in the RS‑232 jack.

Streamers

Link finds Eversolo, WiiM, Bluesound and other BluOS players, Denon and Marantz HEOS speakers, Linn, Lumin and other OpenHome players, Cambridge Audio network players and other UPnP players on your network by itself, one card each. Not there? Panel Setup → Devices → Find Devices. A guest network or a separate network segment hides it from Link.

Too many cards

Every player on the network gets a card. Switch off Show on panel on a player's page to hide that card, or Remove the player.
