Order How it works

Link and the MX200.

Connection

The MX200 has a 3.5 mm RS-232 jack. The supplied 3.5 mm cable fits. Link detects the model by itself and shows the MX200's own inputs and features.

What you control

Volume, mute, inputs, bass and treble, outputs, surround mode, meter lights and power. Settings the MX200 stores per input follow the input.

Volume safety

Volume moves one step at a time, each confirmed by the MX200. A limit you set is enforced in Link, for every phone in the house.

Nothing changes on the MX200

Its remote, front panel and settings work as before. Link mirrors the MX200; turn the knob and every screen follows.

Inputs on the MX200.

12 inputs, one button each. Hide the ones you never use and rename the rest.
