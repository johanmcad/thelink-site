How it works

How it works.

Your network on one side. The control port of the preamplifier on the other.

A bridge

Link sends your taps to the preamplifier in its own control language and shows only what the preamplifier confirms back.

Relative volume only

One step up or down at a time, each confirmed before the next. No gesture can jump the volume.

A volume limit

Set a ceiling. Link enforces it for every phone, and lowers the volume if the limit is moved below it.

A rate limit

At most one step every 150 ms. Lose the connection and stepping stops.

Going above the limit

A separate control, and it locks again after 30 seconds.

Private

No account, no cloud, no tracking. Link only fetches its own signed updates.
